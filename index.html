Almond Croissants

A delicate pastry filled with sweet nutty goodness of almond paste, dusted with powdered sugar, and baked to a golden perfection!

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet of puff pastry
  • 1/2 cup of almond paste
  • 1/4 cup of powdered sugar
  • 1 egg(for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees fahrenheit.
  2. Rollout the ouff pastry into triangles
  3. Spread thin layer of almond paste on each triangle
  4. Brush egg with egg wash and sprinkle with powdered sugar
  5. Finally, bake for 15-20 minutes until the color is golden brown

Sourdough Bread

A Community favorite, boasting a crusty exterior and a soft, airy interior, embodying the classic soughdough charm!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sourdough started
  • 1 1/2 cup of sourdough starter
  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt

Instructions

  1. Mix the starter, water and 3 cups of flour in a large bowl
  2. Add salt and remaining 1 tablespoon at a time until they'r fully incorporated
  3. Knead dough on a floured surface for 15 minutes
  4. Once kneading is done, place dough in a lightly greased bowl, coer it, let it rise for 5 hours
  5. Finally, shape dough into a loaf, and let it rest for 30 minutes
  6. Preheat over to 450 degrees fehrenheit and bake for about 30 minutes until it's golden brown

Classic Carrot Cake

An homage to tradition and familial bonds, enriched with the natural sweetness of carrots and a crunch of walnuts, it holds a special place in my heart as it was my late grandfather's favorite. Every year on my grandfathers birthday, I bake this cake to honor his memory and the bond we shared over the love of baking.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flower
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup vegatable oil
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups grated carrots
  • 1 cup crushed pinnaple
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit
  2. Mix together flour, sugar, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl
  3. Stir in the oil and eggs until smooth
  4. Fold in carrots, pinnaple, and walnuts, then pour batter into greased basking pan
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes
  6. Make sure you allow cake to cool before frosting with cream cheese frosting

Tips and Tricks

  • Measure ingredients accurately
  • Allow eggs and butter to reach room temperature before baking
  • Rotate the baking tray halfway through the baking time for an even bake
